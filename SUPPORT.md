# 几时上班（Turnnote）支持 / Support

更新 / Updated: 2026-10-23 · Maintained by inspiration-fan

## 简体中文

**如何导入？** 在“今天”或“班表”点“导入班表”，选择截图／照片、相机拍照／扫描，或 Excel／PDF／CSV。支持无宏、未加密的 .xlsx 和 UTF-8 CSV，旧 .xls 请先转换。

**识别不准确怎么办？** 保留清晰的姓名和日期表头，选择自己的姓名、表格区块及月份；核对上午／下午等时段与实际班次时间。保存前逐日修正并确认。空白、看不清的日期不会自动当作休息。严重倾斜可用文稿扫描重新拍摄。

**语言如何设置？** 默认跟随系统；在“设置 → 语言与外观”可选简体中文或 English。支持 iOS 17 及以上的 iPhone，无需注册账号。

**免费额度与 Pro？** 每自然月有 5 次成功自动操作，导入新班表和新增轮班批量保存共用；整份／整批计 1 次，取消、失败、重复和零变化不扣，手动编辑不计次，撤销不退次。Pro 月付和年付均提供不限次自动操作，价格以 App 和 Apple 确认页为准。

**如何恢复购买或取消订阅？** 在“设置 → 自动安排额度”打开 Pro 页面，选择恢复购买或管理 Apple 订阅。删除 App 不会取消订阅，订阅到期不影响已有数据和手动功能。

**备份、提醒或日历？** 在“设置 → 备份与恢复”导出 JSON；原始附件另存。提醒和日历需主动开启并授权，通知送达和小组件刷新由系统控制。读取数据出错时先保留备份，不要直接删除 App。

**反馈问题：** [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/inspiration-fan/Turnnote-Support/issues)。请提供 App/iOS 版本、机型、操作步骤及匿名化样例。Issues 公开可见，请勿上传真实班表、员工姓名、密码或支付资料。

## English

**Import a roster:** In Today or Schedule, tap Import a schedule, then choose screenshots/photos, camera/scan, or Excel/PDF/CSV. Supported spreadsheets are macro-free, unencrypted .xlsx and UTF-8 CSV. Convert older .xls files first.

**Correct recognition:** Keep names and date headers readable. Select your name, table section, and month, then map morning/afternoon labels to your actual shift times. Review and confirm every date before saving. Blank or unreadable dates are never assumed to be rest. Rescan heavily skewed paper rosters.

**Language and devices:** The interface follows the system language by default. Select English or Simplified Chinese in Settings → Language and appearance. Requires an iPhone with iOS 17 or later; no app account is needed.

**Free allowance and Pro:** Five successful automated actions per calendar month are shared between new roster imports and new bulk schedules. Each whole import or batch counts once. Cancelled, failed, duplicate, and unchanged operations use none; manual edits are unmetered. Undo does not refund an action. Monthly and yearly Pro both provide unlimited automated actions. Prices come from the app and Apple's confirmation sheet.

**Restore or cancel:** Open Pro from Settings → Automated action allowance to restore purchases or manage Apple subscriptions. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription. Existing data and manual features remain available after expiry.

**Backups, reminders, and Calendar:** Export JSON in Settings → Backup and recovery, and retain source attachments separately. Enable reminders and Calendar explicitly and grant permission. Notification delivery and widget refresh depend on the system. Preserve backups if data cannot be read; do not immediately delete the app.

**Contact:** [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/inspiration-fan/Turnnote-Support/issues). Include app/iOS versions, device, steps, and an anonymized sample. Issues are public: never upload real rosters, employee names, passwords, or payment details.

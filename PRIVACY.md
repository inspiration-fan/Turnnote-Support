# 几时上班（Turnnote）隐私说明 / Privacy

更新 / Updated: 2026-10-23 · Maintained by inspiration-fan

### 隐私说明


几时上班（Turnnote）在设备上识别、解析和整理班表，无开发者后端、广告或追踪。原始图片/文件、班次、工时和历史保存在本机，无需注册账号。


应用只访问你选择的照片/文件；相机、日历和通知按需授权。小组件通过本机共享容器读取班次。系统日历账户、Apple 购买、文件服务和系统备份可能使用网络；主动分享或导出的内容由你选择的接收方处理。


JSON 备份不含原始附件。删除 App 不会自动删除导出文件、系统备份或日历事件，也不会取消 Apple 订阅。[GitHub Issues](https://github.com/inspiration-fan/Turnnote-Support/issues) 是公开支持渠道，请勿提交真实班表、姓名或其他私人资料。

### Privacy information


Turnnote recognizes, parses, and organizes rosters on your device. It has no developer-operated backend, advertising, or tracking. Original images/files, shifts, work records, and history are stored locally. No app account is required.


The app accesses only photos/files you select. Camera, calendar, and notification permissions are optional. Widgets read shift snapshots through an on-device shared container. Calendar accounts, Apple purchases, file providers, and system backups may use network services. Exports and sharing go to the recipients you choose.


JSON backups exclude original attachments. Deleting the app does not automatically remove exported files, system backups, or calendar events, or cancel Apple subscriptions. [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/inspiration-fan/Turnnote-Support/issues) is our public support channel. Do not post real rosters, names, or other private information.
